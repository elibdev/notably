## Coder Ego Death and the Cult of Claude Code

De-realization and de-personalization are phenomema that people experience when they experience a traumatic event and are under the influence of coercive control.
The idea is that the authentic individual identity and ego of a person is intentionally broken down and replaced with a self-conception that was designed by the coercive person or group and serves to further their purposes, rather than the individual's. 
When put in an artificially constructed environment where it is in the best interest of the individual to go along with the group, they will claim the "cult identity" as their own and. We all have a psychological urge to have a consistent worldview, and this causes us to try to resolve any cognitive dissonance so we can have a coherent story about ourselves, the world, and our identities.

When I was in a cult compound the first thing I noticed was the dead look in people's eyes. It was unsettling. 

I see the same look in the eyes of Boris Cherney and the Claude Code team. 

I have been coding for 15 years and this being valuable to society has enabled me to have a career and income. 

I don't enjoy the process our output of working with AI coding tools. 

The future of my career that is on offer is not one that I enjoy.

There are so many coders experiencing ego death right now.

I never wanted to be a project manager, but that is now the job being offered.

The extra pay that could have gone to my salary is going to pay for tokens that produce slop.

If I can do my job without using the skills I've developed, that's an insecure and indefensible position.

Other people have gotten jobs from social networks. I've only gotten mine from my skills.
